Security & data protection measures

Chat exports and compatibility birthdates are processed in your browser. ChatLens does not upload chat content or generated reports to its backend. Account authentication, usage counts and support requests are handled by Supabase.

New locally saved reports use AES-256-GCM encryption with a user-chosen passphrase and PBKDF2-SHA-256 key derivation. Older saved reports and exported PDFs may be unencrypted.

Database row-level access policies and account-linked usage limits are designed to restrict access to your own records. Account changes clear the current report. Content Security Policy and referrer restrictions help limit external connections and information leakage.

No advertising or advertising tracking scripts are included. Internet access is required for account authentication and quota verification. Security measures are not a guarantee against all risks; this beta has not undergone an independent security audit.
