How to use ChatLens

iPhone instructions
  1. Open WhatsApp and the conversation.
  2. Tap the contact name, then Export Chat.
  3. Choose Without Media and Save to Files.
  4. Select the TXT or ZIP file below. If ZIP extraction is unsupported, tap the ZIP in Files and select the extracted TXT.
Android instructions
  1. Open the conversation and tap the three dots.
  2. Choose More, Export chat, Without media.
  3. Save the export and select the TXT or ZIP below.

Analysis runs locally in this browser. No chat content is uploaded. This version uses statistical and keyword rules, not AI.

About ChatLens

ChatLens by Vespa Labs helps you explore message activity, reply intervals and language patterns in two-person WhatsApp exports. It uses local statistics and keyword matching, not AI. It cannot determine intent, feelings or relationship compatibility.

Your chat is processed in your browser. Reports are not uploaded by ChatLens. If you save or share a report, protect it as you would the original conversation.
