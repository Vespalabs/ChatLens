    Message share

    Each person's share of messages in these exports.

      How message share is graded
      • Evenly shared: the larger share is up to 60%.
      • Uneven: the larger share is above 60% and up to 75%.
      • Mostly from one sender: the larger share is above 75%.

      Grades describe message volume. They require two participants and at least 20 messages.

      Typical reply time

      Median elapsed time between messages from different senders, within the six-hour conversation window.

        What this report covers

        It covers messages in the selected files. Calls, in-person conversations, and messages outside those files may add context.
